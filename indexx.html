Who We Are

Three Minds, One Call, Divine Harmony.

Bhajan Mandali isn't just a group name; it's our daily reality. We are three dedicated software professionals who have found the perfect balance between high-stakes development and mental tranquility.

Working remotely, we stay synced on an ongoing collaborative call. While we architect databases, debug complex logic, and deploy applications, the background is filled with the soothing, spiritual sounds of Krishna bhajans. It keeps us grounded, focused, and connected—not just to the code, but to a higher sense of peace.
